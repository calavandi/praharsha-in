Śrī-kṛṣṇa-karṇa-amṛtam is the only available work of the great poet Līlāśuka (c. 11th cent. ce), who was also known as Bilvamaṅgala. This poetical work is composed of around 330 standalone verses about Bhagavān Śrīkṛṣṇa. It extols a young Kṛṣṇa in some of the finest verses ever composed in Sanskrit—with intricate description, rich imagination, profound thoughts, and mellifluous words. Along with the original Sanskrit verses, this book also contains a verse index and details of the poetic meters.
