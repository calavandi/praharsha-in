Gaṅgādevī (c. 14th century CE) was a queen and Sanskrit poetess of the famous Vijayanagara Empire. She chronicles in scintillating Sanskrit verse the story of her husband Kumara Kampanaraya's decisive victory over the Islamic invaders of Madurai.
