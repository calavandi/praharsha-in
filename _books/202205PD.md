Pratibhādūtam is a sandeśa-kāvya that involves travel in time. Kālidāsa, the pioneer of the dūta-kāvya, is the subject. The poet envisions Pratibhā (‘genius’ or ‘inspiration’) as the messenger to send a message to Kālidāsa. The first half of the work contains descriptions of onnoisseurs of Kālidāsa’s poetry. The meeting of Pratibhā with Kālidāsa and the message conveyed to him are depicted in the second half. The Sanskrit poem is accompanied by a Kannada translation.
