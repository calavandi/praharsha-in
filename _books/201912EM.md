This book includes a translation of the 1972 Kannada classic Mahābhāratada Bèḻavaṇige and seven essays on various topics related to the Mahābhārata and the Gītā. Mahābhāratada Bèḻavaṇige tells the story of preparation of the Critical Edition of the Mahābhārata by V S Sukthankar and his team.
