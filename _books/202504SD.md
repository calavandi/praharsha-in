Sandhyā-darśana is an invaluable resource to those interested in Upanayana and allied anuṣṭhānas. For the first time, it presents a refined and systematic explanation of Gāyatrī-japa, Sandhyā-vandana, and other daily rituals of the Vedas, based on the authority of the śruti, sūtra, and principal smṛtis. It contains a reasoned exposition of all the daily duties of a brahmacārī. Several topics are clearly explained and their relevance has been given in a friendly, question-and-answer format.
