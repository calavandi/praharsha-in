Śiva. Rāma. Kṛṣṇa. Three great heroes of the Indian ethos. Revered as gods and upheld as ideals by countless generations from Greater India. Śiva is an ideal for the life of an individual. Rāma serves as a model for family living. Kṛṣṇa’s life is a great teaching that shows us how to live in society. Śatāvadhānī Dr. R Ganesh offers a new perspective to these heroes, who epitomize right speech, right conduct, and right living."
