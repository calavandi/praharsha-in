This is the first instance in the history of Sanskrit literature where the formal discipline of ‘Gūḍhapāda’ (a type of citrakāvya) has been applied to an extended narrative of such magnitude. The Gūḍhapāda-rāmāyaṇam is accompanied by a Sanskrit commentary and English translation.
