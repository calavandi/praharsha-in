The cultural history of a nation encompasses the timeless ethos of a society undergirding events and vicissitudes. To understand a society’s character, we must appreciate the far-reaching contributions by outstanding personalities. In this perspective, the present work comprises biographical sketches of eminent personges from Karnataka of mid-twentieth century.
